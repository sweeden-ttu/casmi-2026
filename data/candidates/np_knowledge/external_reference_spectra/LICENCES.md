# Public spectral library terms

Checked 2026-10-20. This dataset redistributes no paid or access-controlled library. Record provenance and licences must accompany derived spectra; the licence of the harmonisation code does not replace source-data terms.

| Source | Terms and inclusion policy | Official evidence |
|---|---|---|
| MassBank Europe / MassBank-data | The mandatory record `LICENSE` field is authoritative. Preserve `AUTHORS`, `COPYRIGHT`, `PUBLICATION`, accession and release. Include CC0 and CC BY records in the general-use stage; exclude NC/SA/unknown records from that stage. A missing version in `CC BY` remains unspecified, rather than being relabelled 4.0. | [Record format, section 2.1.5](https://github.com/MassBank/MassBank-web/blob/dev/Documentation/MassBankRecordFormat.md#215-license); [releases](https://github.com/MassBank/MassBank-data/releases) |
| MoNA | Database content defaults to CC BY 4.0. Explicit record/source exceptions take precedence; imported records with unclear or conflicting terms are excluded. Preserve submitter, record ID, original source links and any explicit licence metadata. MoNA software LGPL is separate from the content licence. | [MoNA content licence](https://mona.fiehnlab.ucdavis.edu/documentation/license); [downloads](https://mona.fiehnlab.ucdavis.edu/downloads) |
| GNPS | Direct reference contributions default to CC0. Imported collections require separate source-term verification. Use direct collection metadata to select; do not apply CC0 to an aggregate indiscriminately. Exclude propagated/suspect annotations from the measured-reference stage. | [GNPS reference library documentation](https://ccms-ucsd.github.io/GNPSDocumentation/browselibraries/#license); [download catalogue](https://external.gnps2.org/gnpslibrary) |
| MSnLib | The requested DOI endpoint resolves to versioned Zenodo record 21105617 (published 2026-07-01), whose metadata specify CC BY 4.0. Free redistribution/adaptation is permitted with attribution and indication of changes. Use that exact version's terms, independently of the older MassIVE CC0 declaration. Only explicit MS2 records from NIH natural-product and TargetMol natural-product exports are eligible. The `_ms2` and `_msn` files mix record types: pseudo-MS2 pools marked `ALL_MSN_TO_PSEUDO_MS2` are excluded per record, rather than excluding or accepting a whole file by its name. | [Versioned files and licence](https://zenodo.org/records/21105617); [dataset paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-025-02813-0); [earlier MassIVE declaration](https://massive.ucsd.edu/ProteoSAFe/dataset.jsp?task=676a38e2dd574a15905e807d78cf1e57) |

MSnLib versioned creators: Corinna Brungs, Robin Schmid and Tomas Pluskal. MassBank original contributing authors and GNPS PI/data collector/submitter are retained per record. MoNA's original `author` metadata and submitter are both preserved. The first record inspected in the public MoNA LC-MS/MS export carries CC BY-NC; such an explicit exception overrides the database default and is excluded.

The bounded download selection is MassBank release 2026.03; GNPS community plus 13 explicitly direct natural-product collections; MoNA's complete LC-MS/MS export dated 2026-10-20 00:56:39.961 UTC; and the four NIH/TargetMol MSnLib natural-product exports. This does not claim an exhaustive download of every GNPS collection. Known NIST/METLIN/HMDB mentions and unclear original-source imports are conservatively excluded rather than being granted the host repository's default licence.

## Attribution and modifications

The proposed derivative retains authors and source identifiers per record. Changes comprise parsing, unit/field normalisation, removal of stereochemistry followed by canonical tautomer generation for the scorer key, peak sorting/normalisation and exclusion of duplicate or ineligible records. Molecular formula and canonical SMILES are recomputed with the specified RDKit version. These operations do not imply endorsement by library contributors.

Validated retained delta licence text: CC BY 4.0 — 510 spectra; CC0 1.0 — 438; CC BY — 403; CC-BY — 33. The last two version-unspecified strings are retained literally. Full per-record attribution is in the Parquet; all 24 source-file MD5/SHA-256 receipts and dates are in DOWNLOADS.csv and SOURCE_MANIFESTS.json. Selected MSnLib files added zero retained delta records after overlap/conflict screening, but remain credited as an analysed source.
